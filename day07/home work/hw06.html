Dogs

Dogs (Canis lupus familiaris) are the most popular pet on the planet, living in roughly one-third of all households worldwide. As the very first animal to be domesticated by humans over 15,000 to 30,000 years ago, they evolved from an ancient, now-extinct species of Eurasian wolf. Through thousands of years of selective breeding, they have adapted uniquely to human behavior, earning them the title of "man's best friend".
