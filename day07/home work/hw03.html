Giraffe

Giraffes are the tallest living land animals and largest ruminants on Earth, native to savannahs, grasslands, and open woodlands across Africa.• : Adult males grow up to 5.5 to 5.7 meters (18 to 19 feet) and weigh up to 1,930 kg (4,250 lbs); females reach about 4.5 meters (14.8 feet) and weigh up to 1,180 kg (2,600 lbs). • Neck Vertebrae: They possess 7 cervical vertebrae, the same number as humans, though each bone is elongated to nearly 30 cm (12 inches). • Tongue: A prehensile, dark purple-black tongue grows 45 to 50 cm (18 to 20 inches) long, used to strip leaves from thorny branches. • Heart & Circulation: An 11-kg (24-lb) heart with muscular walls maintains high blood pressure, while special vascular valves (rete mirabile) prevent fainting when they lower their heads. • Ossicones: Both sexes have hair-covered horn-like structures called ossicones. • Coat Pattern: Every individual has a unique spotted coat pattern that aids in camouflage and heat regulation.
