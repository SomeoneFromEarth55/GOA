Michael

The highly anticipated Michael Jackson biopic, titled Michael, was released in theaters globally on April 24, 2026, and went on to make cinematic history by becoming the highest-grossing biopic of all time after crossing $977 million at the worldwide box office. The film was directed by Antoine Fuqua (Training Day), written by John Logan (Gladiator), and produced by Lionsgate alongside Graham King (Bohemian Rhapsody) and the Michael Jackson Estate.The biographical film tracks the meteoric rise and complex personal life of the King of Pop. It chronicles his journey from a child star in the 1960s fronting the Jackson 5 in Gary, Indiana, to his global solo superstardom up through his historic Bad World Tour in the late 1980s. The narrative explores his immense creative drive, his dazzling stage choreography, and his turbulent relationship with his demanding father, Joseph Jackson.
