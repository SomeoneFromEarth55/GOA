Sea lions

Sea lions are amphibious marine mammals belonging to the pinniped family Otariidae (eared seals). Known for their intelligence, playful behavior, and loud barking, they are highly adapted to life both in the open ocean and on rugged coastlines. There are six surviving species globally—including the well-known California sea lion and the massive Steller sea lion—though they are entirely absent from the North Atlantic Ocean. Sea lions are among the most cognitively advanced marine mammals. They can learn a simplified form of sign language, recognize individuals after years of separation, and demonstrate long-term memory capabilities comparable to elephants. Because of their trainability, intelligence, and deep-diving skills, the U.S. Navy Marine Mammal Program trains sea lions to detect underwater mines, retrieve equipment, and locate unauthorized divers.
